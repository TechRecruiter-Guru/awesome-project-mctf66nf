## General Outreach Best Practices

### Timing
- **Best days to send:** Tuesday–Thursday (avoid Monday/Friday)
- **Best times:** 8–10 AM or 2–4 PM in recipient's timezone
- **Follow-up cadence:** Days 3, 7, 14 (max 3 touches before pausing)

### Email Mechanics
- **Subject lines:** Keep under 50 characters; avoid "meeting", "intro", "opportunity" (spam filters)
- **Length:** 150–250 words max (executives skim; get to value prop fast)
- **CTA:** Single, specific ask ("20-minute call next week") beats vague "let's connect"

### Personalization Checklist
Before sending, customize:
- [ ] Reference recent company news (acquisition, product launch, funding)
- [ ] Mention recipient's specific role/responsibilities (not generic "I saw your profile")
- [ ] Connect your solution to THEIR stated priorities (not generic pain points)
- [ ] Include social proof relevant to their industry/company size
- [ ] Adjust tone to role (CEO = strategic; CPO = product; CLO = risk/compliance)

### A/B Testing Ideas
- **Subject Line A:** "Solving the [Company] compliance gap — AI hiring documentation"
- **Subject Line B:** "[Mutual Connection] suggested I reach out re: AI hiring compliance"
- **Subject Line C:** "Quick question about [Company]'s AI hiring roadmap"

Test with 2 similar contacts and use the higher-performing variant for the rest.
