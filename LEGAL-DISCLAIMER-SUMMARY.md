## **WHY THIS IS CRITICAL:**

You're selling **technical documentation services** (audit trail recovery), but buyers might think you're providing **legal/compliance advice**.

**Risk:** Practicing law without a license = Big legal problem

**Solution:** Comprehensive disclaimer that clearly separates your technical service from legal advice.

---

## **WHAT THE DISCLAIMER SAYS:**

### **1. NOT LEGAL ADVICE (Crystal Clear)**

```
Defensible Hiring AI provides TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION and AUDIT TRAIL
RECOVERY SERVICES for AI hiring screening tools.

We are NOT attorneys and do not provide legal advice, legal
representation, or compliance consulting services.
```

**Translation:** "We're tech people, not lawyers. Don't come to us for legal advice."

---

### **2. OUR SERVICE DEFINED**

```
Our Institutional Memory API and audit trail recovery services help you
DOCUMENT AND ORGANIZE TECHNICAL RECORDS related to your AI screening tools.

We create documentation packages that help you respond to inquiries, but
we do not interpret laws, provide compliance opinions, or advise on
legal strategy.
```

**Translation:** "We organize your records. We don't tell you what the law says."

---

### **3. CONSULT YOUR ATTORNEY (Explicit Instruction)**

```
All companies using AI hiring tools should consult with qualified
employment law attorneys regarding:

• Compliance with AI hiring regulations (NYC Local Law 144, CA AB 2013, etc.)
• EEOC guidance interpretation and application
• Response strategies for complaints, investigations, or audits
• Legal requirements for bias audits, notifications, record retention
```

**Translation:** "Hire a real lawyer for legal stuff. Seriously."

---

### **4. WHAT WE DO vs. WHAT LAWYERS DO (Side-by-Side Comparison)**

**✅ We Provide:**
- Technical documentation services
- Audit trail recovery and organization
- Documentation templates and frameworks
- Technical record-keeping systems
- Response preparation assistance

**⚖️ Your Attorney Provides:**
- Legal advice and representation
- Compliance opinions and interpretations
- Legal strategy for EEOC responses
- Defense in litigation or investigations
- Regulatory compliance guidance

**Translation:** "Stay in your lane (tech). Let lawyers stay in their lane (law)."

---

### **5. NO ATTORNEY-CLIENT RELATIONSHIP**

```
Use of our services does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Nothing on this website, in our documentation, or in our communications
should be construed as legal advice.

Any information we provide about laws, regulations, or compliance
requirements is for GENERAL INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY and should be
independently verified with your legal counsel.
```

**Translation:** "We're not your lawyers. Don't expect lawyer-client privilege."

---

### **6. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY**

```
You are solely responsible for ensuring your AI hiring practices comply
with all applicable federal, state, and local laws.

Our documentation services are designed to help you maintain organized
records, but compliance decisions must be made in consultation with
qualified legal professionals.
```

**Translation:** "If you get sued, that's on you. We just organize your records."

---

### **7. NO WARRANTY OR GUARANTEE**

```
We make no representations or warranties that our documentation services
will prevent regulatory action, satisfy compliance requirements, or
guarantee any particular outcome in legal proceedings.

All results depend on your specific circumstances and the decisions you
make with guidance from your legal team.
```

**Translation:** "We're not guaranteeing you won't get sued. Results may vary."

---

### **8. BOTTOM LINE (Clear Summary)**

```
📌 BOTTOM LINE:

We help you recover and organize your AI hiring audit trails.

Your lawyers help you comply with the law and defend against claims.

USE BOTH.

Our service complements legal counsel—it doesn't replace it.
```

**Translation:** "You need us AND a lawyer. We're partners, not replacements."

## **WHAT THIS PROTECTS YOU FROM:**

### **Legal Risks Avoided:**

1. **Unauthorized Practice of Law**
   - Risk: State Bar says you're practicing law without license
   - Protection: Explicit disclaimer that you're NOT providing legal advice

2. **Buyer Misunderstanding**
   - Risk: Buyer thinks you're their compliance consultant
   - Protection: Clear statement of what you DO and DON'T provide

3. **Liability for Compliance Failures**
   - Risk: Buyer doesn't comply with law, blames you
   - Protection: "Your responsibility" section + no warranty clause

4. **Attorney General Investigations**
   - Risk: State AG investigates for consumer protection violations
   - Protection: Clear disclosure of service limitations

5. **Professional Liability Claims**
   - Risk: Buyer sues for "bad advice"
   - Protection: No attorney-client relationship, no guarantee of outcomes
