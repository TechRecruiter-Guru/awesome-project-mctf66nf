## 2. Recruiting Engine

### Pipeline Beyond Job Boards

Single Grain's strongest untapped sourcing asset is Eric's combined audience of 1M+ podcast listeners. A listener who opts into growth/marketing content is a pre-qualified cultural signal.

**CEO Brand Pipeline (zero incremental cost):**
- 15-second hiring drops at the end of relevant Marketing School episodes
- Monthly "we're building this" LinkedIn post tagged #SGHiring — reshared by Eric's network of agency founders and operators
- YouTube video: *"What it's actually like to work at an AI-native agency"* — Eric narrates, I produce; evergreen candidate magnet
- Newsletter section: "Who we're looking for this quarter" — transparent, specific, no corporate speak

**Sourcing by Role Tier:**

| Tier | Channel | Volume |
|------|---------|--------|
| IC (Paid, SEO, Content) | Gem sequences, podcast referrals, Slack communities | 15–20 sourced/week |
| Senior IC / Lead | Direct outreach to ex-Wpromote, ex-Directive, ex-in-house F500 marketing | 5–10 targeted/week |
| Manager+ | Eric's warm network, employee referrals, retained search if critical path | Warm only |

**Referral Program:** $2,500 per hire that passes 90 days. Paid in cash. Announced at all-hands. Tracked in Ashby. At 60 people, this closes 20–30% of open roles at near-zero sourcing cost.

### Beat Claude — Redesigned

**Problem with current state:** Ad hoc timing, no rubric, creates candidate anxiety without generating signal quality.

**Redesigned as "AI Collaboration Challenge":** Reframe from competition to collaboration. Candidates receive Claude's output on a real (anonymized) strategy problem and must do three things: (1) identify where Claude got it wrong or missed nuance, (2) extend the analysis with their own judgment, (3) write the exec recommendation they'd actually send. 48-hour async format. Scored on a rubric: problem framing 30%, AI prompting/critique quality 30%, synthesis and judgment 40%.

This surfaces AI fluency *and* strategic thinking. Candidates who can't do this aren't a culture fit. Candidates who ace it self-select in hard.

### Offer Acceptance: 65% → 85%+

The acceptance gap is almost always a surprise problem — surprise on comp, role scope, or timeline.

- **Comp transparency at screen stage:** Share band at the first call ("This role is $90K–$115K; does that work for you?"). Eliminates 80% of late-stage losses.
- **Pre-close call:** Hiring manager calls the finalist 48 hours before written offer. Surfaces objections while there's still time to respond.
- **"Future state" framing:** Every offer conversation includes a 90-second narrative on what the role looks like in 18 months as TES scales.
- **CEO Loom for senior roles:** A 2-minute personalized video from Eric costs him 10 minutes. It closes deals that comp alone cannot.

### Compensation Philosophy

*"Market-anchored, equity-lite, growth-rich."* We pay 65th percentile vs. agency peers — not FAANG, not apologetic about it. We win on: $1,500/year learning budget, $100/month AI tools stipend, profit-sharing tied to TES revenue milestones (transparent formula, not discretionary), and genuine flexibility. The pitch: join a company where AI makes you more productive — which means higher output per hour, not more hours.

## 3. Performance, Retention & Culture

### Performance Cadence (No Annual Reviews)

| Cadence | Format | Owner | Time |
|---------|--------|-------|------|
| Weekly | 30-min manager 1:1 (Notion template: wins / blocks / mood) | Manager | 30 min |
| Monthly | 5-question pulse check in Charlie HR | Me (automated) | 3 min async |
| Quarterly | Performance snapshot: competency ratings + goals check-in | Manager + IC | 30 min |
| Annual | Comp review + leveling calibration (cross-dept session) | Me + leadership | Full day |

Quarterly snapshots replace annual reviews. They're fast, structured, and create the continuous signal needed in an agency environment where priorities shift monthly.

### Compensation Bands (Internal, Posted Transparently)

Benchmarked via Radford agency data + Levels.fyi proxy for adjacent tech roles. Refreshed annually.

| Level | Title Example | Band |
|-------|---------------|------|
| L1 | Associate (0–2 yrs) | $55K–$70K |
| L2 | Practitioner (2–4 yrs) | $70K–$90K |
| L3 | Senior (4–7 yrs) | $90K–$115K |
| L4 | Lead / Principal (7+ yrs) | $115K–$145K |
| L5 | Director | $145K–$185K |

Bands are posted on the internal Notion wiki. Radical transparency reduces poaching risk — when people know where they sit and what promotion looks like, they stop shopping.

### Retention Strategy

**Burnout signals before they become departures:** Claude analyzes monthly pulse data + tenure correlation and flags risk patterns to me. I run "stay interviews" at 6 months — not exit interviews, which are always too late. Three questions: *What would make you leave? What keeps you here? What's one thing we could do better?*

**Structural burnout mitigation:**
- No-meeting Fridays (protected by default, not aspirationally)
- Client load caps by role (Senior IC: max 4 active clients; published in Notion)
- Mandatory minimum PTO: 10 days/year, tracked and surfaced quarterly

**AI upskilling as retention:** Monthly internal "AI Office Hours" — 45 minutes, cross-functional, showcasing how teammates are using AI to do better work faster. This converts the AI mandate from a threat into a career accelerator. People who feel their skills are compounding don't leave.

### Culture During TES Transition

Running two business models simultaneously creates identity confusion. The antidote is deliberate dual-track communication:

- **Town halls:** Every all-hands covers both "Agency wins this month" and "TES milestone progress" — both are celebrated, neither is subordinate.
- **Hire for TES-readiness from day one:** Add this question to every senior interview: *"If this service you're delivering became a product, how would you build it?"* Filters for systems thinkers.
- **Celebrate efficiency gains publicly:** When an AI workflow saves 10 hours/week, make it a Slack win, not a quiet optimization. Reframes AI adoption as status, not threat.

## 4. 90-Day Plan & Metrics

### The Single Highest-Leverage Thing: Data Infrastructure First

Before strategy, before brand, before anything — ATS up, all roles loaded, pipeline visible. Without data, every opinion is a guess and every hire is a gamble. This takes 3 days. It unlocks everything else.

### Milestones

**Day 30:**
- Ashby live; all open roles with scorecards and structured interview kits
- Comp bands drafted for all current open roles
- Beat Claude rubric redesigned and shared with hiring managers
- Onboarding wiki skeleton in Notion
- First employer brand content live on LinkedIn

**Day 60:**
- 2–3 offers extended using new comp-transparent process
- First cohort through structured onboarding (30/60/90 check-ins firing)
- Baseline pulse survey completed — first engagement NPS on record
- Time-to-fill trending toward 35 days
- Referral program announced at all-hands

**Day 90:**
- Time-to-fill ≤35 days (from 45–60)
- Offer acceptance ≥78% (from 65%)
- 90-day retention ≥85% for hires under new process
- Quarterly review cadence live for entire team
- CEO monthly People Signal dashboard running

### KPI Framework

| Category | Metric | Target (90 days) |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Recruiting | Time-to-fill | ≤35 days |
| Recruiting | Offer acceptance rate | ≥78% |
| Recruiting | Pipeline-to-offer conversion | Baseline established |
| Recruiting | Source mix (% referral / % outbound) | Referral ≥25% |

| Retention | Regrettable attrition rate | Baseline + trend |

| Performance | % team with active goals set | ≥80% |
